RAT W06 - corrected print-in-place pack, version 2
Units: mm. Geometry unchanged from W06; print layout corrected.

Use RAT-W06-PIP-all-hardware.stl for the complete plate, or RAT-W06-PIP-carrier-with-captive-holders.stl for just the articulated assembly. The carrier and BOTH camera holders MUST print together, with each ball already inside its socket. Keep their relative positions locked. Do not separate, independently drop, or auto-arrange those three shells. Import them as one object/assembly if prompted. Do not boolean-union the balls into the sockets.

The clips and baseplate are separate on the plate. A combined file without the unchanged baseplate is also included. Do not import individual files alongside the combined file unless you want duplicates.

Both joint gaps are 0.333165 mm in the CAD model, unchanged by the single rigid print-layout transform. The arm-oriented carrier layout and flat clips are preserved. Enable supports where the assembly needs them, but exclude support from the ball/socket gaps so the joints can release. Inspect the sliced joint clearances before printing. Joint release and hardware fit have not yet been physically tested.

All exported STLs pass watertightness, winding, shell-count and self-intersection checks. The captive assembly intentionally contains three separate closed shells in their assembled positions. Electronics, ribbons and skull are not printed in this pack.

This pack replaces the earlier W06 pack with detached holders. Do not use that earlier layout.
