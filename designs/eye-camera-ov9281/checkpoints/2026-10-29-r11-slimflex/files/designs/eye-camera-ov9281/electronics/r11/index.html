Parts and remaining release gates

All 104 fitted packages map to 31 purchasing lines. Known single-unit list prices sum to $24.42, but this excludes the unpriced sensor, two obsolete capacitor codes, custom board, assembly and optics. It is not a total product price.

  • Supply: the sensor lacks a qualified stocked source; multiple exact parts have backorder or lifecycle warnings. Flash has conflicting distributor listings.
  • Power: including the filter's resistance tolerance, DVDD passes the published typical-load screen but falls to 1.135 V at twice typical load, below its 1.14 V minimum. Resolve the required load envelope and delivery margin.
  • Manufacturing: qualify the actual laminate, filled/capped laser vias, local layer removal, supports and assembly process.
  • Optics and operation: select and qualify close-focus IR optics, establish CSI timing, then test real capture, USB power/throughput and thermal behavior. Current firmware is snapshot bring-up, not UVC or continuous recording.
