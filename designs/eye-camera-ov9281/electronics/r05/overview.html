R05 · board design in progress · not ready to manufacture

The camera head now has a defined connection to the controller.

We added the connector, matching solder lands and a short flex draft to the actual KiCad files. Both boards together will make one infrared USB camera.

A simplified view of the saved layout. The flex now has 24 copper conductors. Most wiring on the two rigid boards is still unfinished.

Added

A low-profile locking socket on the controller and a soldered connection on the head. The head avoids a second connector housing.

Checked

All 24 pin assignments and nominal end alignments match. Both attachments fit. Existing components and routed connections are preserved.

Still unfinished

Board wiring, the flex ground reference, optics, IR illumination and working capture firmware. No camera has been fabricated or tested.

Size remains provisional: 76 mm laid flat, including the 30 mm gap. The 12.5 mm-wide flex draft is wider than the Pupil reference. The flexible body thickness is not yet specified for manufacture.

Next: finish the electrical paths. Select the board/flex layer construction, then complete power, ground and camera-signal routing. That is the work still separating these editable drafts from a buildable PCB.

Inspect the actual saved copper and pinout

Red and blue are copper on opposite sides, shown together. Overlapping end contacts belong to separate assembled pieces. This inspection view is not a single PCB to manufacture.

Schematic checks pass. PCB checks still report incomplete connections: 195 on the controller, 93 on the head and eight ground connections on the flex, plus six open camera escapes. No physical mating or signal-integrity test has been performed.

Each pin goes straight through: controller J102 → flex J301/J302 → head J201. The reserved LED connections do not yet have a finished driver.
