What works on the computer

Both ESP32-P4 firmware builds succeed. The host software decodes grayscale packets and saves PGM images with timestamps and provenance. The sensor variant requests one RAW8 snapshot at a time; it does not yet stream 120 fps or appear as a UVC webcam.

Software loopback uses generated patterns, not an emulated camera or a real eye image. No hardware has been flashed, powered or tested.
