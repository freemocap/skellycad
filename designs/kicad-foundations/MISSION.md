# Mission: electronics that fit the hardware

## Why
Learn to build a camera module with a four-pin JST connection carrying USB,
alongside the mechanical designs in SkellyCAD. The indicator board is a brief
tool demonstration; focus on camera electronics and FreeCAD integration.

## Success looks like
- Explain the difference between a circuit connection and a physical placement.
- Inspect and revise a native KiCad schematic and PCB made with Python assistance.
- Hand off a reviewed board to FreeCAD and preserve matched revisions.

## Constraints
- Use Python and file-based tools; do not control the user's desktop.
- Teach one meaningful concept at a time, following the FreeCAD course's rhythm.
- Keep repository organization and tooling work small.
- No quizzes or prediction prompts. Use four broad stages in one rendered HTML guide.

## Out of scope
- A general integration framework before the first working teaching example.
