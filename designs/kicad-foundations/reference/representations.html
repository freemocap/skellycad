From connection to physical part

Symbol
The schematic representation, with electrical pins.
Net
Pins that are electrically connected.
Footprint
The pad and hole pattern where a physical component attaches.
PCB layout
Board outline, placed footprints and copper connections.
3D model
Geometry used to view the component and check its occupied space.
