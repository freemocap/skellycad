# Protruding dummy camera lens

Supersedes the recessed insert in lens-m5-r02. Retains the scaled 6.3 mm holder from lens-m5-r01. Adds an exposed screw barrel, a wider scalloped lens head and a convex fake optical face above the holder lip. Units: mm.

The holder rim remains 2.85923 above its mounting base. Chosen visual dimensions: barrel diameter 4.84, head diameter 5.8, head spans 3.7 to 4.35, convex lens apex 4.45. Projection is 1.59077 above the holder rim. These lens dimensions are illustrative, not new caliper measurements. An approximate external helical groove is visible on the exposed barrel. Export is a fused single-piece black PLA dummy, not a working adjustable optical assembly.

Run build.py with pinned FreeCAD Python and open open.FCMacro through the existing desktop listener. Outputs are under generated/eye-tracker/shared/lens-m5-r03. Validation checks valid single solid, closed mesh and lens projection. Physical fit and slicing remain unverified. Earlier revisions are preserved.
