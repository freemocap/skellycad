OV9281 + ESP32-P4 · packaging study R01

Smaller, lighter — with the mass accounted for.

The target is less than 1.000 g for the complete module, excluding the external tether. The sensor head, controller, inter-board flex, optics, illuminators, board-side connector and retention stay inside that total.

Selected direction: C — SLIM.flex. The user chose the better-documented construction on 2026-10-28. Its small size increase is acceptable. Decision record. The original comparison below remains evidence; B is no longer the preferred direction.

The result

The aggressive layout fits a 24 × 12 mm controller and 12 × 12 mm camera head. Its estimated nominal mass is 1.10 g. The construction based on a published thin-flex stackup, with separate local stiffeners, needs 26 × 12.5 mm / 12.5 × 12.5 mm and estimates 1.27 g.

The subgram target is still open. Low scenarios are below one gram, but neither nominal design meets it. These are unbuilt packaging studies, not newly routed or functional cameras.

R10 estimates 2.18–3.92 g (nominal 2.95 g). The Pupil model's controller is 30.85 × 9 mm, head 15 × 6 mm and free ribbon 32.85 × 4 mm. Its ~0.7 g mass is user-reported; the weighing boundary is not confirmed. Printed model volumes are not camera mass.

Still to qualify

Replacement parts and real weights; a manufacturable stackup and new routing; 10–20 mm IR focus; holder threads and strength; illumination, power and temperature; USB behavior; cable construction and physical testing.

The 8 mm ribbon is a space reservation, not a demonstrated routed interconnect. The new holder's axial overlap drops to 0.47 mm with 1 mm lens extension; thread retention is not established.

Recommendation and cost

Proceed with C as the selected development direction. Resolve actual component choices, weights and construction constraints before rerouting. A is useful as a lower-complexity comparison, but it misses the lightweight target substantially.

No supplier quote has been obtained. Compare equal assembled quantities and separate tooling, fabrication, assembly, parts, inspection, yield and shipping. A is expected to be least disruptive; B/C require premium fabrication and assembly. No vendor has been contacted.
