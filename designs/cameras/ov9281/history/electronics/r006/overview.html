What still needs engineering

Dense sensor and processor pad escapes, the remaining power and camera signals, fabrication-approved layer construction, optics, infrared illumination and capture firmware. The head still has eight isolated sensor ground pads; five controller 3.3 V pads are also isolated.

The 0.60 mm rigid boards and 0.10 mm flex are explicit copper/dielectric assumptions. Current capacity, signal integrity, heating, bending, mechanical mating and image quality have not been demonstrated. FreeCAD contains real exported substrates and copper; component bodies and optics are absent.

The flat assembly is still 76 mm long, with a 32 × 20 mm controller, a 14 × 14 mm head and a 12.5 mm-wide flex. Miniaturization remains unfinished.

Evidence: saved-file validation, added routes and unresolved pads. Layer strategy follows Espressif’s layout guidance; this draft does not yet meet every layout recommendation.
