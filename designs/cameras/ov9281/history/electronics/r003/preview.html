Front: controller on the left, sensor on the right

The large square pad array is the ESP32-P4. The USB JST sits at the controller’s left edge. On the head, the sensor sits inside a reserved lens area; adjacent boxes reserve room for infrared emitters.

Back: the supporting electronics

Flash memory, regulators and many small resistors and capacitors occupy the controller’s back. The head’s back carries its oscillator and sensor support components.

What this draft establishes

99 footprints fit within these provisional outlines, and 408 pad-net assignments match the source circuit. Both boards are provisionally 0.6 mm thick with four copper layers. The flattened length is 76 mm, so further reduction is needed to approach the original 60 mm goal.

Not ready to manufacture. There are no routed traces. The P4’s 0.15 mm pad gaps trigger 103 clearance violations under the current 0.20 mm rule; fabrication capability and escape routing need qualification. Optics, LEDs and the flex connector are reserved areas, not selected parts.
